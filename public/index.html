What Recall can do for you

  • Researches the live web

    Searches current sources and links them in the answer. Under every reply you can see how long it took, how many searches ran and which sites it checked.

  • Remembers your conversations

    Ask about an earlier chat by day or topic, like "What did we discuss on Tuesday?", and it finds it and quotes what was actually said.

  • Fact-checks what it told you before

    Ask "is that still right?" and it re-checks its earlier answers against today's sources, then marks each point Changed Unchanged Couldn't verify or No baseline.

  • Learns how you like to work

    Tell it your name, where you're based or how you like answers ("short, with bullet points") and it remembers. You can see, edit or delete everything in Your profile.

  • Says so when it doesn't know

    If it has no record of something, or can't confirm it, it tells you instead of guessing.

  • Keeps your data with you

    Your chats and profile are stored on this computer. Only the questions sent to the model and to search leave it.
